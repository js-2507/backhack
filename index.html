Connection Guide & Compatibility

Wi-Fi Option: Connect phone to Wi-Fi network Posture-ESP32 (Password: Posture2026) and open http://192.168.4.1.

BLE Option: Select the Bluetooth tab and tap 'Connect'. You can keep normal phone internet active!

  • Android: Works in Chrome, Edge, and Samsung Internet.
  • iPhone / iPad: Safari does not support Web Bluetooth. Please open this page in the free Bluefy or WebBLE browser app from the App Store.
  • BLE Apps: You can also connect via nRF Connect or Serial Bluetooth Terminal with Nordic UART UUIDs.
